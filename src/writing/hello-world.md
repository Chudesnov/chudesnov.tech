I never really got the habit of writing regularly as an adult. The last time period I consistently and proactively posted through (rather than just reactively liking and retweeting something) was probably 12 years ago on my personal [diary.ru](https://diary.city) blog, which was before I got my first real engineering job. As soon as that happened, 90% of the words I type are messages to coworkers or code comments, and I rarely get the impulse (or the attention) to write something outside of that.

This site is an attempt (not the first, likely not the last) to get back into the habit, this time in English (which is now that 90% I mentioned above). I am still *thinking* mostly in my native language, which is Russian, but the idea of producing any long form content in Russian is probably even scarier now that I’ve effectively left that language space outside of some remaining group chats
